The thesis

Intelligence — biological or artificial — is compression.

Brains and networks both build predictive world models and improve by minimising prediction error. Shannon entropy ≈ cross-entropy loss ≈ cortical free energy.

Everything else is a substrate's way of doing compression better.

01 · Maths

Why learning works

  • Compression = understanding. No learning without inductive bias.
  • Depth is exponential, width is not. Lazy vs rich training.
  • Scaling laws, then the data wall. Grokking: memorise first, understand later — and you can engineer which one you get.
Slide 5 / 14
02 · Deep learning canon

What the transformer era inherited

1943–2017: perceptron → backprop → CNN / LSTM → RL → self-supervision.

Three non-negotiables for depth: gates, normalisation, identity paths. The 2012 explosion was a conjunction — algorithm × data × compute — not one jump.

Slide 6 / 14
03 · LLMs

The stack that won

  • Pretraining = capability. Post-training = behaviour.
  • RLHF aligns behaviour — and fails in known ways.
  • RLVR (2025) trains reasoning with verifiable rewards.
  • Test-time compute is a second scaling axis: a dial at inference, not just training.
Slide 7 / 14
03 · LLMs, limits

Scale is not a strategy by itself.

  • Reasoning collapses past a complexity frontier.
  • Knowledge lives badly in weights — retrieve it instead.
  • Superposition: more concepts than neurons.
  • Evaluation is the meta-problem of 2025–26.
Slide 8 / 14
04 · Neuroscience

The parts catalog backprop lacks

  • Dopamine = prediction error. Hippocampal replay = consolidation. Sleep = renormalisation.
  • Global workspace = bottleneck + broadcast.
  • Local learning rules that work without backprop.

These are mechanisms to steal, not metaphors.

05 · Frontiers

What comes after next-token prediction

  • World models: plan in imagination, act in reality.
  • Active inference: one objective for perception, action, curiosity.
  • Post-transformer zoo: Mamba, KAN, diffusion LMs, Titans — each encodes a different bias.
  • Neuro-symbolic hybrids own compositionality. Agents are now a stack, not a demo.
